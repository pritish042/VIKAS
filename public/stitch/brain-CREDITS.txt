VIKAS modifications: retained cortex, cerebellum and brainstem; removed hidden
systems, source materials and metadata not required for rendering; simplified
mesh and retained Draco compression; normalized and merged at runtime;
applied custom violet/blue/lavender physical material and lighting;
created a model-rendered transparent preview. No anatomical parts are
separated or deformed for the interaction. Surface glows and connection
lines are decorative and do not represent measured neural activity.

This is an educational anatomical illustration, not a clinical model.
